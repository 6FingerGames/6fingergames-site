About 6 finger games

An independent game studio from Georgia

6 finger games is a small independent studio run by Davit T. in Georgia. We make games, and this website is where we put them: free games you can play in your browser, and games for the PC.

Why "6 finger"?

A normal hand has five fingers. Five is the standard. The sixth finger is the one that does not fit the standard, and that is exactly how we like to work: we break the frame and try the thing nobody asked for.

How it started

I have been making games since 2008. My first big project was 9 Gramm, a first-person shooter built on CryEngine, which was the best engine of its time. It was funded by a Georgian restaurant owner who believed in the idea. Six months in, his main business ran into trouble, he took his share out of the studio and sold it, the funding stopped, and the project froze. It was a hard lesson about how fragile game projects are when they depend on one source of money, and it is one reason 6 finger games is built to stand on its own feet.

How we make games

Different games are made with different tools. Over the years we have worked with Unreal Engine, Unity and Godot, and we use Adobe Audition for sound. We also use AI assistants (Claude, DeepSeek and Antigravity) as helpers: some for code, some for graphics, some for sound. The ideas, the design decisions and the final say on every game stay with us.

We are also building our own 2D engine, the Symbiosis Engine (we call it Simbo). It is young today, and the plan is for it to grow, over time, into a serious and polished engine for 2D games.

A favourite

The project closest to my heart is HARD STEEL. Many years of effort and patience have gone into it, and that is why it matters to me more than anything else we have made.

What we want this site to be

We want 6fingergames.com to be a gaming portal without digital junk: quality content, a tidy environment, easy navigation, and games that are simply a pleasure to play. No flood of low-effort clones, no tricks to keep you clicking. Every game here is picked and finished by hand.

We also ask new players a few questions when they create a profile (country, preferred language, the kind of games you like). We use the answers for one reason only: to learn who we should make games for and which languages to add. We never sell or hand over this information, and you can read the details in the privacy policy.

What comes next is a secret for now. Come back and see.
