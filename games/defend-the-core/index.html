About DEFEND THE CORE

In the middle of the screen floats a tiny glowing child, and the dark is closing in. What you control is a shield of light that turns around the child, following your mouse or your finger, so you can meet each shadow before it arrives.

Block a ghost and it bursts into smoke and crystals; let one through and the child's light grows dimmer. The shadows come in many kinds: quick wisps, darting thieves, swaying wailers, splitters that break into two, heavy brutes, and huge bosses with many hands. A new wave arrives every twenty seconds, and each one is thicker than the last.

Crystals from every ghost you stop pay for upgrades: a wider shield, a faster spin and more light for the child. You can also choose to watch an optional ad to unlock the Twin Shield and the Starburst, or to restore the light once when it fades. How many waves can you hold?

Questions

How do I move the shield?
Move the mouse or drag a finger and the shield turns to face it. On PC you can also use the arrow keys or A and D.
What kinds of shadows are there?
Quick wisps, darting thieves, swaying wailers, splitters that break into two, heavy brutes and big bosses with many hands.
What do the crystals buy?
Upgrades: a wider shield, a faster spin and more light for the child.
Does the game have ads?
Only if you choose. An ad never appears on its own and never during play. You can choose to watch one to unlock an upgrade, double your crystals or restore the light once.
