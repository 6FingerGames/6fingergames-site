DEFEND THE CORE upgrades and shadows

Every upgrade level and every shadow, with the real numbers from the game

Every shadow you stop in DEFEND THE CORE leaves crystals behind, and crystals buy upgrades for your guardian between runs. This page lists all five upgrades with every level and price, and all seven kinds of shadow with what it takes to stop them. The numbers come straight from the game.

The five upgrades

Three upgrades are bought with crystals, one level at a time. When an ad is available, the shop also offers each of those levels for free if you choose to watch one. The other two are unlocked only by watching optional ads.

Crystals, 4 levels to buy

Shield Size

A wider guard of light. At level 1 it covers a fifth of the circle around the child; at level 5 almost two fifths. The shield gets a new look at levels 2, 3 and 4.

Crystals, 4 levels to buy

Spin Speed

How fast the shield can swing round to follow your finger or mouse. With the keyboard it turns at 80% of this speed.

Crystals, 3 levels to buy

Light

How much light the child holds, which is how many hits it can take. You start with 3 and can raise it to 6.

Watch 3 optional ads

Twin Shield

A second shield, the same size as the first, guards the opposite side and turns with it. Your ad progress is kept.

Watch 2 optional ads

Starburst

Every 18 seconds a wave of light bursts out of the child and clears every shadow near it. A boss only loses 3 of its hits.

The shadows

A shadow is stopped when it hits the shield; tougher ones bounce back and come again until their hits run out. A shadow that reaches the child takes away some of its light.

Wave 1

Wisp

A small flame of smoke with two white eyes. The most common shadow, and from wave 2 they also come in fans of 3 to 7 from one side.

Hits 1 · Light lost 1 · Crystals 1
Speed: Steady
Wave 2

Thief

A thin, pointed shadow with narrow eyes. It flies straight in more than twice as fast as a wisp.

Hits 1 · Light lost 1 · Crystals 2
Speed: Very fast
Wave 3

Wailer

A tall, screaming figure that sways from side to side on the way in, so it rarely arrives where it started.

Hits 2 · Light lost 1 · Crystals 3
Speed: Slow
Wave 4

Splitter

A round shadow with a white crack down its middle. Break it and it splits into two Minis right next to your shield.

Hits 1 · Light lost 1 · Crystals 3
Speed: Slow
From Splitters

Mini

A small, round shadow that comes out of a broken Splitter, already close and moving quickly.

Hits 1 · Light lost 1 · Crystals 1
Speed: Fast
Wave 5

Brute

A hulking shadow with red eyes. It needs four blocks, and if it reaches the child it takes two lights at once.

Hits 4 · Light lost 2 · Crystals 7
Speed: Very slow
Every 6th wave

Boss

A many-armed shadow with red eyes that arrives on every sixth wave. It stops close to the child and keeps spitting three wisps every few seconds. It has 18 hits on wave 6 and 4 more every six waves after that.

Hits 18+ · Light lost 3 · Crystals 50
Speed: Crawls

How the waves grow

  • A wave lasts 20 seconds, and the next one starts right away.
  • Every wave sends shadows a little more often than the one before, and every wave makes them 5% faster, up to twice their starting speed.
  • New kinds join in order: thieves on wave 2, wailers on wave 3, splitters on wave 4 and brutes on wave 5. Brutes get more common for a few waves after that.
  • From wave 2, fans of 3 to 7 wisps arrive together from one side. This is where a wide shield pays off.
  • Every sixth wave the banner says THE DARK GROWS and a boss arrives. While it lives, the other shadows come a little less often.

What to buy first

  • The first Spin Speed level costs only 60 crystals and lets you meet shadows on the far side noticeably sooner.
  • One level of Light (100 crystals) is one more mistake you can survive in every run.
  • Shield Size is the most expensive line, but from wave 2 the fans of wisps reward every extra degree.
  • Twin Shield and Starburst cost no crystals at all. If you do not mind optional ads, Twin Shield is the strongest single upgrade in the game, because it covers the side you are not looking at.
