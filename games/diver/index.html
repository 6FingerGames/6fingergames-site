About DIVER

DIVER is a free online arcade game where you explore the dangerous depths of the ocean. Play instantly in your web browser on PC, tablet, or phone—no download and no sign-up required.

Take a deep breath and dive in! The controls are simple: hold your finger or mouse to dive, let go to rise, and steer to avoid hazards. The deeper you swim, the higher your score multiplier gets, but the ocean becomes far more dangerous. Navigate through the dark past jellyfish, sharks, and anglerfish to collect coins and hidden treasures.

Catch air bubbles and oxygen tanks to extend your dive. But there’s a catch: your score only counts if you make it back to the surface alive. Push your luck for a massive haul, but don't let your air run out!
