About DIVER

DIVER is a free online diving game. Jump off the boat, take one deep breath and dive into the sea: hold to dive, let go to rise, and steer left and right with your mouse, finger or the arrow keys.

The deeper you go, the bigger your score multiplier and the more dangerous the sea: jellyfish, sharks and anglerfish wait in the dark. Grab coins and treasure on the way, catch air bubbles and oxygen tanks to breathe longer.

Your score counts only once you reach the surface. Push a little deeper for a bigger catch, but come back up before your air runs out.

Free to play

DIVER is completely free. Play it right now in your browser on PC, laptop, tablet or phone: no download, no installation, no account and no ads.
