About DIVER

The surface is bright and safe. Down below it is dark, and that is where the treasure is. DIVER gives you one breath and a simple deal: hold a finger or the mouse button to sink, let go to float up, and steer around whatever swims at you.

Jellyfish drift, sharks cruise and anglerfish glow in the gloom. The deeper you go, the higher your score multiplier climbs, and the angrier the ocean gets. Air bubbles and oxygen tanks stretch the dive a little further, if you can reach them.

The twist is the ending: coins mean nothing unless you make it back to the surface alive. Every dive turns into a small argument with yourself about one more handful of treasure, and the air meter does not care what you decide.

Questions

How do I control the diver?
Hold your finger or mouse button to dive, let go to rise, and steer to avoid hazards.
What happens if I run out of air down there?
Your score only counts if you reach the surface alive, so the dive ends and the haul is lost. Watch the air meter.
Why go deep at all?
The deeper you swim, the higher your multiplier, but the ocean also gets far more dangerous. That is the whole gamble.
