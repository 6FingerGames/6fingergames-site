About FRUIT MERGE 6

Aim, drop, and watch two matching fruits squash together into a bigger one. In FRUIT MERGE 6 you feed the box one fruit at a time, from tiny apples and oranges up the ladder to giant peaches, and every merge brings you closer to a new record.

Chain quick combos for a bigger score, but manage your space and keep the pile below the danger line. Bright colours and juicy effects keep the whole thing cheerful.

When the box overflows, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to clear the large fruits and carry on with the same run. The game is available in five languages: English, Georgian, Russian, Chinese and Japanese.

Questions

How do I make bigger fruit?
Drop two matching fruits so they touch. They merge into the next, bigger fruit and score points.
What ends a run?
Keep the pile below the danger line. When the box overflows the run is over, unless you choose the second chance.
What is the second chance?
If the box overflows you can choose to watch a rewarded ad. It clears the large fruits so you can continue the same run.
