About KICK IN THE BUTTS

One very skinny, very surprised guy needs to go as high as possible, and you are the foot that sends him. Watch the power gauge swing and tap when the needle is in the red: a giant shoe slides in, WHAP, and up he goes.

In the air you steer left and right with the mouse, your finger or the arrow keys while he tumbles, somersaults and splats against the walls. Trampolines, clothesline ropes, spring trees and balloons keep him bouncing, rockets give a boost, angry birds get in the way, and long trails of coins are waiting to be scooped up. Fly high enough and you might reach a space station.

When gravity finally wins, hold to dive head first into the ground. Then spend the coins in the shop: more leg power, and new shoes with springs, spikes or rocket boosters. How far can you launch him?

Questions

How do I get a strong first kick?
Watch the power gauge swing and tap when the needle is in the red. Then a giant shoe slides in and he flies.
Can I steer after the kick?
Yes. Use the mouse, drag a finger, or press the arrow keys or A / D to move left and right while he flies.
What can I buy in the shop?
More leg power, and four kinds of shoes, including ones with springs, spikes or rocket boosters.
Does the game have ads?
Only if you choose. An ad never appears on its own and never during a flight. You can choose to watch one to double your coins, get a free upgrade or unlock the rocket boots, or to relaunch after a high flight.
