About KICK IN THE BUTTS

KICK IN THE BUTTS is a free online arcade game about sending one very skinny, very surprised guy as high as you can. Play instantly in your browser on PC, tablet or phone: no download and no sign-up.

Watch the power gauge swing and tap when the needle is in the red. A giant shoe slides in, WHAP, and up he goes. Steer him left and right with the mouse, your finger or the arrow keys while he tumbles, somersaults and splats against the walls.

Bounce off trampolines, clothesline ropes, spring trees and balloons, grab rockets for an extra boost, dodge angry birds and scoop up long trails of coins. Fly high enough and you might even reach a space station.

When gravity wins, hold to dive head first into the ground, then spend your coins in the shop: more leg power, and new shoes with springs, spikes or rocket boosters. How far can you launch him?

Questions

Is KICK IN THE BUTTS free?
Yes. It is a free online game. It costs nothing to play.
Do I need to download or install anything?
No. The game runs right in your web browser. Open the page and press Play.
Can I play it on my phone?
Yes. Tap to kick and drag your finger to steer. On PC use the mouse, arrow keys or A / D.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your coins and upgrades are saved only in your own browser.
Does the game have ads?
Sometimes. A short ad may appear between kicks, never during a flight, and you can optionally watch one to double your coins or unlock a shoe.
