KICK IN THE BUTTS shop

Every shoe, every leg level, what they cost and how hard they kick

Everything you buy in KICK IN THE BUTTS makes the first kick stronger, and the first kick decides most of the flight. The shop sells two things: leg power, which you level up, and shoes, of which you wear one pair at a time. The two multiply: the kick power of your leg level times the bonus of your shoes. All the numbers below come straight from the game.

Leg power, level by level

Maxing out your leg costs 23,276 coins in all, and level 15 kicks 2.82× as hard as level 1. With Rocket Boots on top, that becomes 4.79×, plus the rocket's push at kick-off.

What to buy first

Because each leg level adds the same 13% of the starting kick while its price keeps rising, the early levels are a bargain and the late ones are not. A few comparisons, using the prices above:

  • Levels 2, 3 and 4 together cost 190 coins and add 39% to your kick. Nothing else in the shop comes close.
  • Once your leg is at level 5 or 6, Spring Shoes (+12% for 250 coins) give more for the money than the next leg level.
  • Once your leg is at level 7, every further level costs 456 coins or more and adds less than 8% to what you already have. Spiked Boots are a good next goal then: they add 22% over sneakers for 700 coins, and the birds start paying you instead of slowing you down.
  • Rocket Boots cost no coins at all, only three optional ads, and they beat every other shoe. If you do not mind the ads, they are worth getting early.
