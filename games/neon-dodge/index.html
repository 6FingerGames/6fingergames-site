About NEON DODGE

Dodge. Survive. Get a high score! Your ship races down a glowing synthwave grid while a storm of neon shapes rains from above.

Each shape has its own temper. Heavy orange squares drop like fireballs, sharp magenta triangles are fast and aim straight at you, and purple hexagons zigzag across the screen, every one dragging a burning trail. You have three hearts, so every move counts.

Lime crystals are your way to score. Grab them one after another to build a combo up to x5, then use that speed to slip back into the gaps. Every twenty seconds the storm speeds up and the screen gets wilder.

Steer with the mouse, drag a finger or use the arrow keys or WASD. Rare emergency rockets can blast away nearby shapes when you double-tap. Reflexes help, but a calm head helps more.

Questions

What do the different shapes do?
Orange squares fall like fireballs, magenta triangles are fast and aim at you, and purple hexagons zigzag with a burning trail.

Collect lime crystals one after another to build a combo, up to x5. Then use that speed to dive back into the gaps.

Does NEON DODGE have ads?
Rarely. A short ad may appear between runs, never during play, and you can choose to watch one to get an extra heart after losing.
