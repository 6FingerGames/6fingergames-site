About NEON DODGE

Dodge. Survive. Get a high score! NEON DODGE is a free online arcade game set deep in a synthwave night sky. Play instantly in your browser on PC, tablet or phone: no download and no sign-up.

Your ship races down a glowing grid while a storm of neon shapes rains from above. Heavy orange squares drop like fireballs, sharp magenta triangles are fast and aim straight at you, and purple hexagons zigzag across the screen, each one dragging a burning trail behind it. You only have three hearts, so every move counts.

Snatch the lime crystals to push your score. Collect them one after another to build a combo up to x5, then use that speed to dive back into the gaps. Every twenty seconds the storm gets faster, and the screen gets wilder.

Controls are simple: move the mouse, drag your finger or use the arrow keys / WASD. Sharp reflexes and a calm head are all you need. How long can you survive, and can you beat your best score?
