ODDLINGS creatures

All forty, from Pib to Stardrake: rarities, eggs and what each one earns

There are forty creatures to find in ODDLINGS, split into five rarities of eight. This page shows every one of them, which eggs can hatch it and how many coins it earns per second. The numbers come straight from the game, so you can plan which egg to save for.

How the numbers work

Each creature has a base income at one star. Every merge that takes it up a level multiplies that income by four, and the ladder ends at eight stars. Merging always pays: two one-star pets earn 2 coins a second between them, while the two-star pet they become earns 4 on its own. An eight-star pet is worth 128 one-star pets of its kind, but it earns 16,384 times as much as one of them.

The album adds a bonus on top: +3% income for every kind you have ever hatched, and +10% more for every kind you have taken to eight stars. A full album is worth +120%, and a full album with every pet at the top is worth +520%.

Egg prices climb as you hatch more and as your income grows, so the prices above are where they start. Tapping an egg while it incubates shaves a little time off. Inside a rarity every creature has the same base chance, though the game leans toward kinds you have not found yet, and now and then toward a twin of a pet already on your habitat, so there is always something to merge.

Notes for collectors

  • Mythic creatures hatch only from the Golden Egg. Each Golden Egg has a 10% chance of a mythic, so about 1.25% for one particular kind.
  • The Wooden Egg can still surprise you: one hatch in a hundred is rare.
  • A duplicate is never wasted. It feeds the pet of the same kind and fills its progress bar, and that progress already counts toward its income.
  • Hatching a new kind pays twice: once as a new pet and once as +3% on everything through the album.
