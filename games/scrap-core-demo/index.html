About SCRAP CORE Demo

This is the real opening of SCRAP CORE, not a cut-down mock-up. You crash onto a notebook page as CoreBro, a robot made of scrap, and the ink on the page starts crawling toward you.

You get the first three waves in full and a taste of the fourth, with the Workshop in between: buy pens, compasses and erasers as weapons, put two of the same together and watch them merge into something nastier.

Then Xenom shows up, a talking blot of living ink that likes to wrap you up and drain your batteries, and the demo stops right there. The full game has all 12 waves, five notebook pages, the ink serpents and the Redline Leviathan waiting on the last page.

Free to play

The demo is free and needs no account. It is made for a computer: move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim and shoot with the mouse, pause with Esc. It does open on a phone and you can steer with your finger, but there are no on-screen controls, so it is much harder to play there.

Features

  • Three full waves and the start of the fourth
  • The Workshop: school supplies that merge into stronger weapons
  • Xenom, the talking ink blot, at the end
  • 10 languages, each fitted by hand
  • No ads, no account

Questions

How much of the game is in the demo?
Waves 1 to 3 in full and the start of wave 4, up to the moment Xenom arrives. The full game has 12 waves and a final boss.
Can I play it on my phone?
It opens, and you can move with your finger, but SCRAP CORE was built for keyboard and mouse. On a computer it plays much better.
Does the demo save my run?
No. Every demo run starts from wave 1, and nothing carries over to the full game.
Are there ads in the demo?
No, none at all.
