About SCRAP CORE

Your ship blew up somewhere above a school notebook. You are CoreBro, a little robot held together with scrap and stubbornness, and you just landed right in the middle of the page. The ink here is alive. And it is hungry.

Every wave spills more ink monsters across the paper: scribbled mites, sneaky hunters that dash at you out of nowhere, and nests that never stop hatching. Keep moving, aim, hold fire, and scoop up the shiny crystals they leave behind.

Between waves, the Workshop is all yours. Pens, compasses, erasers, paperclips, markers and rulers become your weapons. Buy two of the same and they snap together into something bigger, louder and much meaner.

But the page fights back. Ink serpents crawl out of holes in the paper. Xenom, a shiny blot of living ink, chats, laughs at you and wraps you up to sip your batteries dry, so spin like crazy to shake it off. And on the very last page waits the Redline Leviathan: six legs, a cannon, a laser eye and a truly terrible temper.

On every wave, a piece of your shattered transmitter falls somewhere on the page. Find all twelve, fit them back together, switch it on, and call the rescue ship to take you home.

Hand-drawn, a little silly, and surprisingly tough. How far can one small pile of scrap get on a page full of ink?

  • Bosses with real personality: ink serpents, a talking ink blot and a giant war machine
