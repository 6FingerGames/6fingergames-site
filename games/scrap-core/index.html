About SCRAP CORE

CoreBro, a robot made of scrap, crash-landed on a notebook page full of ink monsters.

Survive 12 waves, merge hand-drawn weapons, outsmart a talking ink blot, three ink serpents and a giant boss, and rebuild your transmitter to call the rescue ship home.

Features

  • 12 waves on 5 hand-drawn notebook pages
  • 6 weapons that merge from tier I to IV
  • A boss with an energy shield and a fury phase
  • Awards album and achievements
  • 7 languages
