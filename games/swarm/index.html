About SWARM

SWARM is a free online arcade game where you control the power of gravity. Play instantly in your browser on PC, tablet, or mobile—no download and no sign-up required.

Move your mouse or finger to attract glowing lights and build a massive, orbiting swarm. Your goal is to guide them into a giant black hole before time runs out. The more lights you gather, the stronger your gravitational pull becomes. Use this power to drag heavy spaceships into the void for massive combos and extra time.

Watch out for purple anomalies that will destroy your swarm. Keep your rhythm, build your gravitational field, and see how long you can survive under the Milky Way.
