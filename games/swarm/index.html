About SWARM

Somewhere under the Milky Way there is a black hole that wants feeding, and you are the one holding the gravity. You glide the cursor (or a fingertip) across the sky and every glowing light you touch joins your orbit.

A bigger swarm pulls harder, and that pull is how you drag heavy spaceships off course and into the void. Each ship earns combo points and a few extra seconds on a clock that is always running, so the game becomes a steady trade: gather more lights, or cash in the ones you have.

Not everything out there is friendly. Purple anomalies wreck any swarm they touch, so you learn a rhythm of gather, pull, step away. Levels keep coming and your best result is remembered on your device.

What am I supposed to do with the swarm?
Gather glowing lights, then steer the swarm so its gravity drags spaceships into the black hole. Every ship adds combo points and extra time.
