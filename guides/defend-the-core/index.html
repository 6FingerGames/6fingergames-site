DEFEND THE CORE: player's guide

The shield, the shadows, the upgrades and how to hold more waves

In DEFEND THE CORE a tiny glowing child floats in the middle of the screen and shadows come out of the dark. Your only tool is a shield of light that turns around the child. The game is calm to look at and tense to play. Here is how to hold on longer. You can play the game right here: Play DEFEND THE CORE.

The basics

Move the mouse or drag a finger and the shield turns to face it. Every shadow you block bursts into smoke and drops crystals. Every shadow you let through dims the child's light. When the light fades, the run is over.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: the shield follows your pointer around the child.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or A and D turn the shield.

The shadows

  • Wisps are quick and weak. Sweep them away as they come.
  • Thieves dart sideways and try to slip past. Watch their motion and keep the shield ahead of them.
  • Wailers sway as they approach, so aim for where they will be, not where they are.
  • Splitters break into two when hit, so be ready for the second one.
  • Brutes are heavy and take longer to deal with.
  • Bosses are huge and come with many hands.

Waves

A new wave arrives every twenty seconds, and each is thicker than the last. Do not spend long on one shadow. Often the better move is to turn to the next arrival and come back.

Crystals and upgrades

Crystals from every ghost you stop pay for upgrades: a wider shield, a faster spin and more light for the child. Early on, a faster spin helps most, because it lets you reach shadows that come from the opposite side. Later, a wider shield makes crowded waves easier.

Optional ads

The game may offer optional ads at moments you choose, such as unlocking an upgrade, doubling your crystals, or restoring the light once when it fades. They never appear on their own and never during play.

Quick tips

  • Turn early, not late.
  • Learn the movement of each shadow.
  • Buy spin speed first, width second.
  • Keep the child's light high; it is the whole game.
