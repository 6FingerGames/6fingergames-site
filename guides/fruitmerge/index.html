FRUIT MERGE 6: player's guide

Merging, combos, the danger line and how to use the second chance

FRUIT MERGE 6 is a physics puzzle in a cheerful box: drop fruit, merge matching pairs into bigger ones and keep the pile below the danger line. It is easy to learn, but the pile gets crowded faster than you expect. These ideas help you stay calm. You can play the game right here: Play FRUIT MERGE 6.

How merging works

You drop one fruit at a time into the box. When two matching fruits touch, they merge into the next, bigger fruit and score points. The ladder starts with small fruit such as apples and oranges and climbs to giant peaches.

Controls

  • Aim: move the mouse or finger, or use the left and right arrow keys.
  • Drop: click, tap, or press Space.
  • Pause: press P or Escape.
  • Language: five languages (English, Georgian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese) from the menu.

Building a clean pile

Try to keep the biggest fruit in one corner and build the next sizes beside it, in order. A tidy ladder makes merges happen almost by themselves, while a random pile traps small fruit under big ones.

Do not rush the drop. The next fruit is shown, so plan where it will land before you release it. A fruit dropped on the edge of two others can start a chain that clears half of the box.

Combos

Merges that follow each other quickly count as combos and score more. A good setup is to leave two matching fruits next to each other, then drop a third that triggers a merge and pushes the result into another match.

The danger line

If the pile reaches the danger line and stays there, the run is over. Watch the heights of your tallest columns. When one gets close, merge there first and leave the rest for later.

The second chance

When the box overflows, you may be offered an optional ad. If you choose to watch it to the end, the large fruits are cleared and you continue the same run. The offer is not always available, and you never have to take it. The game plays the same without it.

Quick tips

  • Keep big fruit in a corner.
  • Look at the next fruit before you drop.
  • Aim for chains, not single merges.
  • Merge near the danger line first.
