KICK IN THE BUTTS: player's guide

Controls, tricks, the shop and how to fly higher

KICK IN THE BUTTS looks like a one-button joke, and it is, but there is more going on under the shoe than it seems. This guide covers everything the game does not tell you in the first few kicks: how the power gauge really works, what each obstacle does, where your coins should go, and how to reach the space station. You can play the game right here: Play KICK IN THE BUTTS.

The basics

Every run has three parts: the kick, the flight and the landing. You aim by stopping a swinging power gauge, a giant shoe sends a very skinny, very surprised guy into the sky, and then you steer him while he flies. When he finally falls, you earn coins for the trip and spend them in the shop on stronger legs and better shoes. Then you kick again, and go higher.

Controls

  • Kick: tap the screen, click, or press Space when the needle is where you want it.
  • Steer in the air: move the mouse or drag your finger left and right, or use the arrow keys or A / D.
  • Dive: hold the screen, the mouse button, or Space / Enter / the down arrow while he is falling. Holding makes him drop twice as fast, which is how you make it back to coins and bounce pads quickly.

The perfect kick

The power gauge swings back and forth, and the harder the shoe hits, the higher the first launch. The power is not linear: a kick at 100% flies much farther than one at 80%, because the launch speed grows faster than the gauge. Aim for the red zone at the top; a kick above 92% is a PERFECT! and the game tells you with a green flash and a sound. Do not rush, the needle swings at a steady rhythm, so after a couple of passes you can feel when it is about to peak.

Steering and diving

Gravity in this game is floaty on purpose, so flights are long and you have time to plan. Your horizontal steering is smooth, not instant, so start moving toward a trampoline or a coin trail early instead of at the last moment. Use the dive when you are falling and there is something good below you: holding doubles the fall speed, so you reach the next bounce pad before you run out of height.

What you will meet in the sky

  • Trampolines, clothesline ropes, spring trees and balloons bounce him back up. Each one also leaves a long column of coins above it, so a good bounce pays twice.
  • Rockets give a strong boost upward. They are rare and worth steering toward.
  • Birds bonk him and slow him down. Avoid them, unless you wear spiked boots (see below).
  • Coin trails are your income. Collect them whenever it does not cost you height.

All of the bounce objects live in the lower air, up to about 100 metres. Above that the air is thin, and only coins and rockets appear. That means after the first stretch you cannot count on trampolines: the height you carry from the kick and your last rocket are what you have.

Coins and the shop

When a run ends you get the coins you picked up plus a height bonus of one coin for every four metres you reached, so a taller flight pays even if you grabbed few coins.

Leg power is the upgrade that matters most. Each level adds about 13% to your kick power, and the price rises by half each time (40, 60, 90, 135 coins and so on). It improves every future flight, so it is usually the best purchase early on.

The shoes change how the kick and the landing work:

  • Sneakers: free, plain and reliable.
  • Spring shoes (250 coins): +12% power, and a small extra bounce on landing.
  • Spiked boots (700 coins): +22% power, and they pop birds for 3 coins each instead of being slowed by them.
  • Rocket boots: +70% power and an extra boost at kick-off. These are not sold for coins: you unlock them by choosing to watch three optional ads.

Ads in this game are always your choice. They never appear in the middle of a flight, and you can ignore every one of them and still enjoy the whole game: watching one only gives you a bonus, such as double coins after a run, a free leg upgrade, or progress toward the rocket boots.

Reaching the space station

Very high above the city (about 375 metres, depending on the flight) a space station waits. It is the first big goal, and it is only reachable with a really great flight. Touch it and he bounces off with a shout of "HOUSTON!" and 100 bonus coins. The recipe is simple: upgrade your legs, hit a perfect kick, collect a rocket on the way, and steer carefully through the thin air.

If you keep going, keep your eyes on the sky above your record. There is a surprise up there for the player who flies the highest.

Quick tips

  • Spend your first coins on leg power, not on shoes.
  • A perfect kick is worth more than any single pickup in the air.
  • Start steering early. Late corrections lose height.
  • Dive to reach a bounce pad, not just to fall faster.
  • Save your progress is automatic: coins, upgrades and your best height are stored in your browser, so use the same browser to keep them.
