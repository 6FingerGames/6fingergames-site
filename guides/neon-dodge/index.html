NEON DODGE: player's guide

The shapes, the combo, the rockets and how to survive the storm

NEON DODGE throws a storm of glowing shapes at a small ship on a synthwave grid. You have three hearts and a lot of reflexes to spend. This guide explains what each shape does and how to turn a nervous run into a long one. You can play the game right here: Play NEON DODGE.

The goal

Survive as long as you can and collect lime crystals. Crystals are your way to score. Grab them one after another to build a combo up to x5, then use the speed you have gained to slip back into the gaps between the shapes.

Controls

  • Mouse: move it to steer the ship.
  • Touch: drag a finger on the screen.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD.
  • Rocket: double-tap (or double-click) to blast nearby shapes, if you have one.

Know your enemies

  • Orange squares are heavy and drop straight down like fireballs. They are predictable, so use them as walls to hide behind.
  • Magenta triangles are fast and aim at you. Move early, because they do not forgive late reactions.
  • Purple hexagons zigzag across the screen and drag a burning trail behind them. Stay out of the trail as well as the shape.

Hearts and combos

You start with three hearts, so each hit matters. A good rhythm is to collect crystals while the screen is calm, then hold your position when it gets wild. Do not chase a crystal into a crowd of shapes: a lost heart costs more than a lost combo.

The storm

Every twenty seconds the storm speeds up and the screen gets wilder. The first minute is for learning the shapes, the second for building your combo, and after that it becomes a test of calm. Panicked big movements lose more hearts than small precise ones.

Emergency rockets

Rare rocket pick-ups appear during a run. Each lets you double-tap to blast the shapes around you, and you can hold up to three. Save them for the moment the screen closes in, not for the first crowded wave.

Optional ads

After you lose, you may be offered an optional ad. If you choose to watch it to the end, you come back with an extra heart. No ad ever appears during play.

Quick tips

  • Learn each shape by its colour.
  • Small moves beat big ones.
  • Keep your combo, but never chase it into danger.
  • Save the rockets for real trouble.
