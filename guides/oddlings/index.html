ODDLINGS: player's guide

Eggs, merging, the album and how to grow your habitat faster

ODDLINGS is a relaxed idle and merge game. You hatch small, round, slightly odd creatures, let them earn coins and merge them into stronger ones. It plays well in short visits. These tips help you grow faster without pushing any buttons you do not want to. You can play the game right here: Play ODDLINGS.

The loop

You start with one egg and a few coins. Tap the egg when its timer runs out and a creature appears. Every creature earns coins on its own, second after second, so the more you have the faster your habitat grows. Spend coins on better eggs, more room, extra nests and habitat upgrades.

Merging

Drag a creature onto another one of the same kind. The stronger of the two absorbs the weaker and climbs a star. Two Lv1 pets make a Lv2, and the ladder goes all the way to eight stars. Pets with a teal outline have a twin on your habitat and are ready to merge.

When the habitat is full

A new creature is never wasted. It is always added to your album. If you already own that kind, it feeds the pet and fills a small progress bar on its tile. If you do not, you choose to release it for coins or to swap it in for your weakest pet.

Collecting

There are forty creatures in five rarities: common, uncommon, rare, epic and mythic. Each new one you hatch is stamped into the album and adds a bonus to all your income, and every creature taken to its top level adds a bigger one. Better eggs make the rare ones much more likely, so they are worth saving for.

Leaving the game open or closed

Close the page and your pets keep working for up to four hours, at half of your normal income. When you return, a summary shows how much your creatures earned. There is also a small daily gift and daily quests.

Looks and saves

You can switch between a calm dark look and a bright candy look. Everything is saved in your own browser, so there is nothing to sign up for.

Optional ads

Some boosts can be unlocked by watching an optional ad when one is available. An ad only starts when you press a boost button yourself, and you get the reward only after it finishes. The game plays the same without them.

Quick tips

  • Merge as soon as you have a pair, because stars boost income.
  • Save coins for better eggs rather than many cheap ones.
  • Visit once a day for the gift and quests.
  • Do not release a creature you are one merge away from.
