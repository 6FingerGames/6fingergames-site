SCRAP CORE: player's guide

Waves, enemies, Xenom, the boss, weapons and the Workshop

You are CoreBro, a little robot built from scrap. Your ship crashed onto a torn notebook page. Survive 12 waves of ink monsters, find a piece of the transmitter on every wave and call the rescue ship. Between waves, spend crystals in the Workshop on weapons and upgrades.

You can try the first waves for free in your browser: the SCRAP CORE demo covers waves 1 to 3 and the start of wave 4, up to the moment Xenom arrives. The full game for Windows is on itch.io.

1. Goal

ResultHow
VictoryDefeat the boss, the Redline Leviathan, on wave 12. It drops the last transmitter part, the transmitter switches on and the rescue ship arrives.
DefeatYour life reaches zero, or Xenom drains all your energy.

Waves 1–11 end when the timer reaches zero: the enemies left vanish and you go to the Workshop. A serpent or Xenom stops the timer while it lives: the wave does not end until you beat it.

2. Controls

  • Move: WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Aim: the mouse.
  • Shoot: hold the left mouse button or Space.
  • Pause: Esc.
  • Throw off Xenom: spin the mouse round CoreBro, or press the arrow keys or WASD round in a circle (W, D, S, A).

CoreBro does not shoot on his own: hold fire. Every shot flies exactly to the crosshair, from each gun's own muzzle.

Manual aim (in Settings): when the mouse is still, CoreBro keeps his last direction instead of turning to the nearest enemy.

3. The loop

  • Start, then the opening film (skip it with Skip or Space).
  • A wave: enemies, crystals and a transmitter part.
  • Level up during the wave: pick 1 of 4 upgrade cards.
  • Wave end: results and awards.
  • The transmitter: fit the part you found.
  • The Workshop: buy weapons and upgrades.
  • The next wave, and so on until the boss on wave 12.

You start with 100 life and one weapon, the Bolt Pen I.

Arenas (pages): five different pages, changing on waves 4, 7, 10 and 12. Every stage has its own music.

5. Enemies

  • Mite: chases you and rams. The most common.
  • Spitter: keeps its distance and spits ink shots. From wave 2.
  • Rammer: stops, aims (a red line) and dashes. From wave 4.
  • Nest: slow and tough, hatches little mites. From wave 6.

Enemies grow more numerous and tougher from wave to wave.

Ink Serpents (waves 3, 6, 9)

A 12-segment serpent crawls out of a hole and hunts you. Its head's bite hurts more than touching its body. Now and then it aims and lunges. The green one spits shots, the jelly one hatches mites. The serpents of waves 6 and 9 match your strength: the stronger you are, the tougher they are.

Reward: the fast gun. Pick it up and the Bolt Pen fires 250 rapid shots, then goes back to its own pace. A burst of extra enemies comes for a few seconds right after, so you have something to shoot.

Xenom, the ink puddle (waves 4, 5, 8)

A living blot of shiny ink with angry white eyes and teeth. It talks and mocks you.

  • It crawls on ink legs and reaches for you with a feeler; run, and the feeler stretches and snaps.
  • Close up, it bristles, crouches and lunges. Dodge to the side!
  • If it catches you, it wraps you up. Nothing else can hurt you in there, but you cannot shoot, and Xenom drains your energy (the big gauge over your head). At zero energy CoreBro switches off and you lose.
  • To throw it off: spin the mouse round CoreBro one way (or the arrow keys in a circle), about 6 turns. Stop, and it closes in again.
  • Thrown off, it bursts into 4 bits that crawl back together. While apart they take double damage: shoot them.
  • Low energy makes your guns fire slower; energy refills by itself.

Reward: a battery. It gives back 35% of your max life, all your energy and 8 crystals.

The boss: Redline Leviathan (wave 12)

A huge walking machine with a cannon, with a crowd coming along. Its life and damage match your weapons and toughness, so even a strong build cannot kill it in seconds.

  • Fury: at half life everything stops: it rears up, blows its horn and slams the ground. The shock wave wipes out every enemy and the FURY music starts. From then on it also fires a laser.
  • Victory: a huge explosion, its wreck, and the last transmitter part.

6. Weapons

You can carry 6 weapons. Two identical weapons of the same tier merge into the next tier: I, II, III, IV.

  • Bolt Pen: a fast, precise bolt. Fires 2 and 3 at once on tiers III and IV.
  • Compass Blade: spins round CoreBro and cuts up close.
  • Eraser Cannon: a heavy shot that pierces enemies; enemies it kills are rubbed off the page.
  • Paperclip Arc: an electric arc that hits several enemies at once.
  • Red Marker Mine: leaves an explosive mark on the page.
  • Ruler Beam: a long straight beam that cuts through everything in line.

7. Levelling up

Picking up crystals gives experience. On a new level the game pauses and you pick 1 of 4 upgrades, or skip.

On the boss wave the Magnetic bag is not offered, only useful cards.

8. Crystals and the Workshop

  • Killed enemies drop crystals. A crystal picked up counts at once and also gives experience.
  • Half of the crystals left on the page at the end of a wave go into your bag and come back at the start of the next wave.
  • There are crystal veins on the page too: shoot them for a harvest (bonus crystals) at the end of the wave.
  • The Workshop offers 3 cards: weapons, upgrades or Repair (+24 life). Prices grow 25% each wave.
  • Reroll (the die): new cards; each reroll costs more than the last.
  • Pin: pin a card and it stays when you reroll.

9. The transmitter

On every wave, about 6 seconds in, a transmitter part turns up on the page in a dashed ring: walk into the ring to pick it up. After the wave you fit it into the transmitter. With all 12 parts the transmitter switches on and the rescue ship arrives. The last part drops from the boss.

10. Surprise box

Now and then a gift box appears on the page. Shoot it open and a helping hand comes out: it wipes out a few enemies with a finger or an eraser, or draws a cage round them with a pen. It leaves the boss, serpents and Xenom alone.

11. Style and awards

Style rank: many fast kills raise it: D, C, B, A, S and finally SCRAP!

Awards (flags): there are 8 flags, and the awards card in the menu shows how many you have (for example 4/8).

FlagHow to earn it

Boss and serpent flags have three grades; for three stars, win without being hit once.

12. Difficulty, saving, continues

Difficulty (the menu card with CoreBro): Noob, Normal and For the cool ones. The easier, the weaker the enemies and the less they hurt.

Saving: the game saves itself at the start of every wave and in the Workshop. "Start" from the menu offers the saved place. The browser demo does not save runs.

Continue: after a defeat you can go back to an earlier wave. The price is half your crystals and a weapon tier; the more you give up, the closer to where you fell you come back. Continues: Noob 8, Normal 6, For the cool ones 4. The defeat page shows in red how many you have left. When they run out, the page tells you and a new game starts.

13. Settings

Sound, music, manual aim and the language: English, Georgian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. From the menu you can also open the help, the about page, your awards, the difficulty and the opening film (the play button).
