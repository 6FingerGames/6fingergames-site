HOW TO PLAY

Move your ship with the mouse, your finger (drag) or arrow keys / WASD.

Dodge the neon shapes. You have 3 hearts.

Collect lime crystals. Chain them fast for up to x5 score.

Emergency rocket: double-click, double-tap or press Space twice. Clears nearby shapes. Max 3, no points.

Pause: P or ESC   Mute: M
