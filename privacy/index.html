Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information 6 finger games ("we") and our game SCRAP CORE ("the game") use, and why. We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible.

Who we are

6 finger games is an independent game studio run by Davit.T in Georgia. Contact: 3dstudio@mail.com.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves your progress, settings (such as language, sound and difficulty), earned awards and achievements only on your own computer. We do not receive this information, and it is never sent to us.

The game has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-game purchases and no accounts of its own.

Epic Games Store version

If you play the game from the Epic Games Store, the game signs in with your Epic Games account through Epic Account Services, with your permission. It uses only basic profile information (your Epic account ID and display name), and only to unlock Epic Games Store achievements for you. We do not store this information on any server of ours. Epic Games handles this information under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Stores and websites

If you buy or download the game from a store (such as itch.io or the Epic Games Store), that store handles your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

This website does not use cookies or tracking. It is served by a hosting provider, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Children

The game is made for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your questions and requests

If you write to us by email, we use your message and address only to answer you. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages. For information that Epic Games or a store keeps, please contact them directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
