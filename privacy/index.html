Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information 6 finger games ("we") and our games use, and why. We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible, and we do not sell anything about you.

Who we are

6 finger games is an independent game studio run by Davit.T in Georgia. Contact: 3dstudio@mail.com.

What our games store, and where

Our games save your progress, settings (such as language, sound and difficulty), earned awards, achievements and a few small notes (for example "this hint was already shown") only on your own device. Games you play online in your browser keep this in your browser's local storage. We do not receive this information, and it is never sent to us. If you clear your browser's site data, this progress is gone.

For example, ODDLINGS stores your creatures, coins, album, upgrades, chosen look and a few hint markers in your browser's local storage under the names oddlings_v1, oddlings_theme and oddlings_tips. Nothing else is stored, and none of it leaves your device.

Our games have no in-game purchases. They have no accounts of their own.

Advertising

This website and some of our online games show advertising provided by Google (Google AdSense and Google's ad services for games). Games that use ads follow one rule: ads never interrupt play. They appear only between rounds, or only when you press a button yourself to watch a short ad in exchange for something extra, such as a boost, an extra life or an extra chance. You receive the reward only if you watch the ad to the end, and the game works the same without them.

To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies, and may receive information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are on. Whether ads are personalised depends on your settings and on where you live. We do not give Google any information from your profile or your game progress. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage or turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. If you use an ad blocker, our games still work; the optional "watch an ad" rewards are simply not available.

Fonts

Some pages load text fonts from Google Fonts. When a font is requested, Google's servers receive your IP address and browser details, as with any file loaded from another server.

Player profiles: our promise

Registration is optional. We do not collect this data to sell it or to give it to any third party. Never. It has one purpose only: to learn who we should make games for (girls, boys or anyone else) and which countries need their own language in our games.

We do not care about your real name or surname, and we are not interested in your email or anything private. We only want this site to fit what you need when you visit it.

What is stored where. Your nickname and profile stay in your own browser. When you create a profile, only your anonymous audience answers are also sent once to our database: country, gender (or "prefer not to say"), age group, preferred language, the device you play on, player type and the games you like. They contain no nickname, no email, no password and nothing that identifies you, and they are not linked to your device or to your game progress. The database is hosted for us by Supabase. Profiles are for players aged 13 and over, so under-13 answers are not accepted.

You can edit your profile or log out at any time. Because the answers we receive are anonymous, we usually cannot tell which entry is yours, so we cannot delete a single answer on request; if you want your local profile gone, log out or clear your browser data. If we ever add accounts with an email or a password, we will update this page first.

Epic Games Store versions

If you play one of our games from the Epic Games Store (for example SCRAP CORE), the game signs in with your Epic Games account through Epic Account Services, with your permission. It uses only basic profile information (your Epic account ID and display name), and only to unlock Epic Games Store achievements for you. We do not store this information on any server of ours. Epic Games handles this information under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Stores and websites

If you buy or download a game from a store (such as itch.io or the Epic Games Store), that store handles your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Apart from the anonymous answers above, we do not collect personal data.

Children

Our games are made for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and profiles are limited to players aged 13 and over. Parents and guardians: the ads in our games are provided by Google; you can limit ad personalisation in your device and Google account settings.

Your choices

You can clear a game's saved data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, turn off personalised ads in your Google ad settings, or use an ad blocker. If you write to us by email, we use your message and address only to answer you, and you can ask us at any time to delete your messages. For information that Google, Epic Games, Supabase or a store keeps, please contact them directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
