Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information 6 finger games ("we") and our games use, and why. We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible.

Who we are

6 finger games is an independent game studio run by Davit.T in Georgia. Contact: 3dstudio@mail.com.

What our games store, and where

Our games save your progress, settings (such as language, sound and difficulty), earned awards and achievements only on your own device. Games you play online in your browser keep this in your browser's local storage. We do not receive this information, and it is never sent to us.

Our games have no advertising, no in-game purchases and no accounts of their own.

Player accounts: our promise

Registration is optional. We do not collect this data to sell it or to give it to any third party. Never. It has one purpose only: to learn who we should make games for (girls, boys or anyone else) and which countries need their own language in our games.

We do not care about your real name or surname, and we are not interested in your email or anything private. We only want this site to fit what you need when you visit it.

For now your profile is stored in your own browser on your device. If we ever move accounts to a server, we will update this page first. You can edit your profile or log out at any time, and you can ask us by email to delete your data.

Epic Games Store versions

If you play one of our games from the Epic Games Store (for example SCRAP CORE), the game signs in with your Epic Games account through Epic Account Services, with your permission. It uses only basic profile information (your Epic account ID and display name), and only to unlock Epic Games Store achievements for you. We do not store this information on any server of ours. Epic Games handles this information under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Stores and websites

If you buy or download a game from a store (such as itch.io or the Epic Games Store), that store handles your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

We do not collect personal data.

Children

Our games are made for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your questions and requests

If you write to us by email, we use your message and address only to answer you. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages. For information that Epic Games or a store keeps, please contact them directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
