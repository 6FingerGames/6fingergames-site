SYMBO ENGINE

SYMBO Engine is still in development. The download will appear here as soon as the first version is ready.

A free 2D game engine from 6 finger games (full name: Symbiosis Engine)

Symbiosis Engine is the 2D game engine we are building at 6 finger games. We started it for our own games, and we want to give it to everyone: you will be able to download it for free and make your own 2D games with it.

When you make a game, it is a product of your mind, your imagination, and your thought processes. You put your soul, your time, and your talent into creating something that didn’t exist before. And that is priceless. It’s something you can leave behind; something your children, parents, and relatives can be proud of, saying, “My son made this,” or “My relative made this.” You can live an entire life and leave nothing behind, like billions of other people on this planet. But creating a game means leaving it as a reminder of your existence: proof that you were here and that you were capable of doing this.

When an artificial intelligence makes a game based on your prompt, “make me a game like this,” it’s the exact same thing as typing “free online games” into Google. Neither of those things belongs to you. It’s not your product, it’s not the fruit of your mind, and it wasn’t created by you. Yes, you can fool everyone around you into thinking you made it, but deep down, you’ll know it’s not true.

In our incredibly fast (I would even say catastrophically fast) developing world, everything changes so rapidly that you don’t even have time to process the flow of information about new inventions, let alone try them out.

You can start building a game from scratch, the old-fashioned way, but it might take years of hard work to finish it. On the upside, it will be entirely your product. Just like my game, HARD STEEL: I’ve been building it from scratch for 4 years. Every little detail, every single pixel, is the fruit of my labor. It hasn’t been published yet; the game is 95% finished and almost ready for release, but circumstances haven’t allowed me to launch it just yet.

That is why, in parallel, I’ve been creating a new game engine. But this time, I integrated tools into it that incredibly accelerate the game development process: ready-made templates, blueprints, and integrated AI systems. You can create music right inside the engine, generate sprites, slice them, make animations, modify them, and create new ones. You can write scripts directly in the engine, build long logical blueprints, give enemies ready-made “brains,” or ask the integrated AI for help. It can even create games inside this engine on its own, without your assistance.

But here is the difference: you can watch in real time as the AI does it. You can stop it and fix what you don’t like right on the spot in the editor. Instead of wasting time asking the AI to move an object to another place, you can do it yourself in a matter of seconds. This is what true symbiosis looks like. Today, if an AI agent makes a game, you can’t intervene directly: it just hands you a finished result. But here, you have complete freedom of action: stop the AI, tweak things exactly how you want them, and then press a button so it continues from where you left off.

A game made this way can rightfully be considered your creation, because the AI simply assisted you, rather than building everything entirely on its own.

Download our engine, it is completely free. You can plug in any AI using an API key. All the details and instructions are already prepared to show you how to do it.

And then, just press the “Publish” button, and your game is ready. You can instantly package it to run on any server or export it as a Windows .exe file.

Where it stands today

The engine is in active development. The first public version is not ready yet, so there is no download button on this page. Before we release it, we will go through it carefully ourselves: fix mistakes, test it on real projects and close the security questions. We would rather publish a smaller engine that works properly than a big one that does not.

What it is for

  • 2D games. SYMBO is built for 2D from the start, not as a 3D engine with a flat mode.
  • Blueprints and AI. Visual scripting, with an AI partner you can watch and correct live.
  • Free to download. You will not need to pay or sign up to try it.
  • Made by game makers. We use what we build, so the engine is shaped by the problems we meet while making real games.

Working together with AI

The idea behind the engine is a partnership between a person and an AI. You can connect an AI to the engine through an API, and then you watch it work live, right in the viewport: you see every object it creates and moves. At any moment you can stop it, move an object yourself, fix something you do not like, and let the AI carry on from your change. The human is an equal partner in the work, not someone who only reads the result.

The AI builds the game with blueprints (visual scripting), not with written code. That makes its work easy to read for a person: you follow the logic as connected nodes and can edit it by hand.

Why "Symbiosis"?

Symbiosis is two partners that make each other stronger. For us it means the engine and the people who use it: your games show us what is missing, and every improvement goes back to you.
