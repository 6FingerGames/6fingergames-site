SYMBO ENGINE

A free 2D game engine from 6 finger games (full name: Symbiosis Engine)

Symbiosis Engine is the 2D game engine we are building at 6 finger games. We started it for our own games, and we want to give it to everyone: you will be able to download it for free and make your own 2D games with it.

Where it stands today

The engine is in active development. The first public version is not ready yet, so there is no download button on this page. Before we release it, we will go through it carefully ourselves: fix mistakes, test it on real projects and close the security questions. We would rather publish a smaller engine that works properly than a big one that does not.

What it is for

  • 2D games. SYMBO is built for 2D from the start, not as a 3D engine with a flat mode.
  • Blueprints and AI. Visual scripting, with an AI partner you can watch and correct live.
  • Free to download. You will not need to pay or sign up to try it.
  • Made by game makers. We use what we build, so the engine is shaped by the problems we meet while making real games.

Working together with AI

The idea behind the engine is a partnership between a person and an AI. You can connect an AI to the engine through an API, and then you watch it work live, right in the viewport: you see every object it creates and moves. At any moment you can stop it, move an object yourself, fix something you do not like, and let the AI carry on from your change. The human is an equal partner in the work, not someone who only reads the result.

The AI builds the game with blueprints (visual scripting), not with written code. That makes its work easy to read for a person: you follow the logic as connected nodes and can edit it by hand.

Why "Symbiosis"?

Symbiosis is two partners that make each other stronger. For us it means the engine and the people who use it: your games show us what is missing, and every improvement goes back to you.
